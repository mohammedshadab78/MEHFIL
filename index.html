About the Mehfil

A mehfil is a gathering — a late-night assembly of voices, verses, and quiet listeners. This archive is a digital re-creation of those evenings: dusty cassettes, warm tungsten light, the hum of an old amplifier, and the raw, unpolished beauty of qawwali.

Est. 1992. Analog soul. Digital airwaves. Every track lives on this shelf, played directly from local audio files — no streaming, no algorithms, no interruptions.
